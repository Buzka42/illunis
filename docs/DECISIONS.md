# Decisions

Recorded when a door closed.

## Store

On-device JSON (`AppStore` in `app/lib/core/db/store.dart`), not Drift.
Every derived row carries `journalEntryId` so REVERT deletes the page and
what it produced. Drift is still the later store; do not start it until
the JSON file is the bottleneck.

## Dictation

The journal mic uses the device recognizer (`speech_to_text`). Tap starts
a listen, tap again stops. Words land in the writing line for review —
they are not submitted until the person hits done. If the recognizer is
missing, denied, or this platform cannot listen, the tap focuses the
field the way it used to.

Android: `RECORD_AUDIO` only (no Bluetooth, `androidNoBluetooth`). iOS:
microphone + speech usage strings. Windows may listen when the OS
recognizer is present.

Latin-fold (`app/lib/core/i18n/fold.dart`) so dictated Polish hits the
same food and lift aliases. `foldSpoken` also turns a comma between
digits into a decimal (`82,4 kg`, `5,2 km`) *before* the page is split
on `,`, so lists (`eggs, bread`) still split and mass does not become
`4 kg`. Unlisted Polish noun declensions (genitive `-a`, `-ego`, accusative
`-ę`/`-e`) fall back to nominative stems in `lookupFood`. Sentence
punctuation (`.`, `!`, `?`) and Polish `potem` split chunks cleanly.
Listen uses the device locale, 60 s, pause 4 s.

## Estimates

Food and activity guesses land `confirmed: false` and stay out of totals
and strain. CONFIRM / DROP sit on the writing-line dock, not in the page
column. Trivial drinks (≤ 10 kcal) and explicit `kcal` in the line are
already logged. KEEP LOCAL is per page and is cleared after a successful
log so it does not stick onto the next one. PREV/NEXT is catch-up: an
unstamped line lands on that day. A `yesterday` / `last night` stamp
reveals that day but does not turn catch-up on, so the next unstamped
line stays on the clock day. Unconfirmed estimates from yesterday still
sit on today's writing-line dock. Number words (one–ten / jeden–dziesiec)
scale food and sleep hours. Meals split on `and` / `,` / `with` / ` z ` /
` i ` unless the phrase is a named compound (`chicken with rice`,
`ryz z kurczakiem`). `zapiekanka` is not a `z` split.

## Sleep

A quality-only line (`slept poorly`) does not invent hours; it marks last
night. `went to bed at 23:10` is a bedtime, not a night, until `woke at
6:45` finishes it. The hours belong to the morning they woke. A wake with
no bedtime does not invent a start. An evening bedtime logged before
noon is last night. `last night` on food or movement is yesterday; on
`slept 7h` it stays the morning they said it.

## Food calories (v1)

The journal uses a local table of generic household foods
(`app/lib/core/nutrition/foods.dart`). OpenFoodFacts vs USDA FoodData
Central is **deferred**. Scoring them honestly needs ~40 real dictated
meals. When that sample exists, pick by hit-rate on generic foods and
replace the table — not before.

## Dashboard honesty

Absent figures are omitted (INTAKE, WORK, ENERGY, MASS, RECOVERY). SLEEP
and STRAIN stay: unknown is `—`, a rest day with a log is strain `0`.
7-day energy balance is null when intake is missing on ≥ 3 of 7 days.
The timeline hairlines are **today's** pages, not the selected journal
day. Local digest is one instrument sentence, ≤ 140 characters, never
advice; an AI digest is kept across later confirms.

## Wearables

The `health` package is skipped so Windows desktop stays buildable.
Health Connect / HealthKit is Phase 3.

## Night sky (catalog)

Stars are the HYG v3 catalog (Hipparcos + Yale Bright Star + Gliese,
CC-BY-SA 2.5, David Nash / astronexus), bundled, `V ≤ 6.0`, J2000 RA/Dec,
drawn as Canvas points at catalog magnitude. Regenerated by
`tool/stars/generate.py`. The hashed field in `sky.frag` was removed so
Sirius is not drawn twice.

Locked with that pass:

- No GPS. Observer is the Settings home.
- No star names or constellation lines in v1.
- No runtime fetch. The catalog ships in the app.
- Procedural Milky Way band remains until a texture is justified.
- Precession only if a window/Stellarium check is off by more than 0.5°.
- Night look is south unless a risen, lit moon is up. The sun's set
  azimuth is not a night heading.

Spec: `docs/SKY_CATALOG.md`.

## Settings

Long-press JOURNAL / DASHBOARD. The sheet scrolls, capped at 72% of
height, so the Blade's system nav does not clip DELETE. Import is **JSON
from the clipboard**; CSV is export-only.

## Backup (decided 2026-09-10, sideload channel)

`AndroidManifest.xml` stays silent on `allowBackup`: Google auto-backup
of the full `illunis.json` is the only automatic durability story, and a
no-account journal needs one. Accepted consequence: `aiConsentAt` and
`ageAtLeast16` migrate with the file on device transfer, while clipboard
import deliberately excludes them. Revisit with a per-file split before
any Play release.
