# Illunis — Privacy policy (DRAFT, unhosted, 2026-09-10)

> Host this text, then put the URL in the Play listing and the Settings
> sheet. It describes the tree as of the September 2026 sideload builds.

**Illunis** is a personal health journal. There is no account and no
developer-side database of your entries.

**What stays on your device.** Everything you log — journal text, sleep,
activity, food, weight, and the summaries computed from them — is stored
only on your device, in the app's private storage. Deleting it is done
inside the app: Settings → DELETE ALL DATA. Device auto-backup (if your
system enables it) may copy the app file as part of your own backups.

**What leaves your device, and when.** Nothing leaves by default. If you
turn on ALLOW INTERPRETATION (and confirm you are 16 or older), the text
you submit and the day's figures are sent to the developer's relay
server, which forwards them to an AI provider for interpretation and
returns the result to your phone. Entries you mark KEEP LOCAL are never
sent. You can turn interpretation off at any time; earlier results already
returned to your phone stay in your local history until you revert or
delete them.

**Microphone.** The dictation button uses your system's speech recognizer
to turn speech into text in the entry field. Audio is not recorded or
stored by the app.

**Location.** Not collected. Your sky position comes from a home location
you set yourself in Settings.

**Children.** Interpretation requires confirming you are 16 or older.
